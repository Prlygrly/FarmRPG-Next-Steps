# How much pets bring

- Each pet has 12 items in three groups of four: level 1 items from the start, level 3 items from level 3, and level 6 items
  from level 6. Every pet's list: [pet data](/data/pets.md).[^wiki-long]
- Average of **each** unlocked item per day, per pet:[^wiki-long]

  | Pet level | Each item per day | Groups it brings |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1 | 6 | level 1 items |
  | 2 | 12 | level 1 items |
  | 3 | 9 | level 1 and 3 items |
  | 4 | 12 | level 1 and 3 items |
  | 5 | 15 | level 1 and 3 items |
  | 6 | 12 | level 1, 3 and 6 items |
  | 7 | 14 | level 1, 3 and 6 items |
  | 8 | 16 | level 1, 3 and 6 items |
  | 9 | 18 | level 1, 3 and 6 items |
  | 10 | 20 | level 1, 3 and 6 items |

  No known top level.[^wiki-shop]
- A level 6 pet gives about 12 of each of its 12 items a day, so about 12 a day of a level 6 item like the Seal's Frost
  Snapper Shell: small next to a Grand Mastery, but free.[^wiki-long]
- The Owl at level 6 or higher brings **double Honey** (since 2024-06-24); no other pet doubles anything.[^wiki-long]
- Pets roam town and bring items every hour, one item per pet level each hour. They hold each item up to your inventory
  cap (every item, every pet) until you collect them on the All Pet Items page; collecting past your cap voids the extra,
  so check first.[^wiki-shop][^player]
- Pets stop gathering after 48 hours offline.[^wiki-shop]

# Prices

- Levels cost 10x the one before: level 2 100,000 silver, level 3 1M, level 4 10M, level 5 100M, level 6 1B, level 7 10B,
  level 8 100B, level 9 1T, level 10 10T.[^wiki-shop]
- Buying a pet (silver): Cat 10,000; Dog 100,000; Squirrel 400,000; Owl 1M; Swine 5M; Snake 10M; Lemur 35M; Baboon 50M;
  Frog 75M; Hedgehog 100M; Armadillo and Spider 125M; Pet Rock, Penguin and Miner 250M; Bird and Fox 400M; Green and
  Red Dragon 1B; Bear 2B; Blue Dragon 3B; Seal 4B; Capybara 5B; Strange Onion and Critter 10B. Pets unlock by
  requirements shown on each pet's page.[^wiki-shop]
- Seasonal pets (Polar Bear, Hummingbird, Shark, Wolf) cost 1,000 silver plus an item in your inventory and only gather
  part of the year.[^wiki-shop]
- Level 6 pets are a secondary source of some super rares (Seal: Frost Snapper Shell; Lemur: Amber; Snake: Skull Coin;
  Pet Rock and Fox: Diamond): see [super rares](/mechanics/rare-drops.md).[^wiki-long]
