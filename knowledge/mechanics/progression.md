# Before the Tower

- The four main skills are Farming, Fishing, Crafting and Exploring. **4x99** (all four at 99) is needed for the major questlines; exploring is usually slowest.[^rkm-tips]
- At **4x90** the "A Towering Investment" (ATI) questline unlocks; finishing it opens the [Tower](/mechanics/tower.md).[^rkm-tips][^wiki-rare]
- Save **600 Honey** for ATI III before any other honey quest.[^rkm-tips]
- No need to unlock Glacier Lake or Jundland before ATI is done; they use ATI resources. RILL is the usual order for opening places.[^rkm-tips]
- For new players: fishing at Farm Pond by hand with bait restores 5 stamina per fish; silver early on comes from crafting and the Sawmill (see Sawmill Silver); Craftworks gives no crafting XP, so it suits low-XP items.[^rkm-tips]
- Keep pigs leveling rather than slaughtering them early: higher-level pigs are worth far more silver later.[^rkm-tips]
- Mastery rewards (silver, gold, perk points) matter more than AK before the Tower; see [mastery](/mechanics/mastery.md).
- Silver is very valuable until Truffles ("The Smell of a Fun Guy"); see [silver](/mechanics/silver.md) for Sawmill Silver from Crafting 20.

# In the Tower (player guide)

| Tower levels | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1-50 | Get to 4x99. Start "The Smell of a Fun Guy" (Truffles: big silver; 1,000 cannons, wishing well items). "Unlikely Pair"/"Unexpected Visitor" and "A Wall in the Way"/"The Fall of Water" alongside; T50 Runecube artifacts help with rare items. |
| 51-100 | Finish those; raise Buddy to friendship 50 for "Secretly a Society Summons You" (SASSY). T100 gives +2,500 inventory and gold: reinvest in the farm. |
| 101-150 | "Corn Of Interest", "Excuuuse Me Farmer", "Archeology Requires Knowhow", "A Better Juice" (better Veggie Juice), "Pie Inversion Experimentation" (Pie Machine). |
| 151-200 | "Monster Hunter" (double bugs, needed before STARMAP/PAMRATS), EMF, COI, "An Interesting Lunch", "My Cabbages", "Strange Companions", "Peculiar Endings", "A Way Back", "Far Away"; "Fitness Friends Fit Forever" opens Gary's Crushroom. |
| 201+ | STARMAP (after EMF) and PAMRATS (after An Interesting Lunch), then PSA; "Effigy Of Friendship"; "The Book of Dewstar Mastery". |
| 235+ | "Distant Illusions" (needs 12.5k inventory to start, 15k to finish). |
| 275+ | "Daily Dairy" (cows also make Horns). |

- Keep the Feed Mill producing: Gold Feed builds up over time even at full inventory, and quests need lots of it.[^dorfy]
- Corn Of Interest tips: potatoes from Grab Bag 01 (Forest Pond), corn and salt from Large Chest 03 (Pirate's Cove); keep 245 of each battery/engine part for PHR rewards.[^dorfy]

# Planner implications

Players without the Tower shouldn't see Tower talk; for them the most valuable things are quests and leveling toward 4x90/4x99.
