# Why silver matters

Silver is hugely valuable until "The Smell of a Fun Guy" is done and Truffles are available (Truffles make big silver). Before then, crafting and selling or fishing and selling are the main sources.[^player]

# Sell price

- Sell price = base price x (1 + sell perks %) x mastery bonus (x1.1 once Mastered, x1.2 once Grand Mastered).[^wiki-mastery]
- Worked example: Iron Cup, base 165, Grand Mastered or better, sells for 337: 165 x 1.7 x 1.2 = 336.6, so that player's sell perks add up to about 70%.[^player]
- Mega Mastery seems to add no further sell bonus (x1.3 wouldn't fit the example).[^player]

# Sawmill Silver (from Crafting 20)

1. Build the Sawmill and raise Boards per hour as far as you can.
2. Craft all Boards into Wooden Planks, then Planks into Sturdy Shields; sell all the Shields.
3. Reinvest all the silver into more Boards per hour, until at least 150-200 an hour. Repeat.
4. At 150-200 Boards an hour this makes 5-10 million+ silver a day.

- Needs a 60% sell bonus: Negotiator I-IV (perk-point perks) and Fertilizer I (Farm Supply gold perk).[^wiki-sawmill]
- Crafting 46: add Fancy Pipes (Wood from the Sawmill + Stone from the Quarry); Iron Depot helps. Boards go to Shields and Wood to Pipes.[^wiki-sawmill]
- Crafting 70: Lanterns make significantly more (needs Glass from exploring); Craftworks helps here.[^wiki-sawmill]
- Late game: Boards, Wood and Stone over 1,000 an hour can make hundreds of millions a day (needs a big inventory).[^wiki-sawmill]
- Fishing at Lake Minerva, Large Island and Pirate's Cove also makes lots of silver: explore the Forest for Antlers, make nets, fish your best unlocked zone.[^wiki-sawmill]

# Best silver later (player guide)

- 4x99 with Pirate's Cove / Glacier Lake: explore the Forest, craft Large Nets, fish Pirate's Cove or Glacier Lake (best, especially after the T70/T90 artifacts).[^badymaru]
- Easy mode: Large Nets in the best fishing zone you have (from Lake Minerva on, each zone beats the last).[^badymaru]
- Still leveling Crafting and Exploring: explore Whispering Creek, craft Sturdy Bows and Garnet Rings (Mushroom Paste + Steel); Lanterns are an easy alternative.[^badymaru]
- Only if the ingredients are spare: Linked Lantern, Blue Purse, Red Shield, Purple Bag, Crossbow, Fancy Drum, Fancy Guitar.[^badymaru]

# Spending silver (rough order)

| Milestone | Suggestions |
|---|---|
| First | Sawmill Silver, then Storehouse / inventory growth |
| 10 million | Quarry at Crafting 46 (Fancy Pipes); +2 daily Storehouse upgrade; max the Orchard for your inventory; 10M crop row (10M Farming XP); keep production buildings up; Farmhouse, Vineyard; level 4 Owl (Honey); Hay Field (Twine -> Rope -> Fishing Nets) |
| 100 million | +2 daily Storehouse; Orchard; 100M crop row; Owl to 5; Pig Pen, Flour Mill, Feed Mill, Raptor Pen, Steelworks |
| 1 billion | Max Orchard (count Forester perks); +2 daily Storehouse; level 6 pets (Bird: about 12 Large Nets a day); 1B crop row; Kitchen (500M Farming XP, unlocks Cooking) |
| 10 billion | Catch up the above; Tower artifacts; Strange Onion pet (mega seeds, about 100M a day); Capybara; level 7 Bear |
| 100 billion | Tower 90/100/110 artifacts; crop row 11 (Mega Farm merit badge, 300 AK); Storehouse +16 |
| 1 trillion+ | Quests that need trillions: "Flexible Spending", "A Fool And Their Money" (5T each, cosmetic), "Problems Start Arising" |

More fruit means more juices, which means more stamina: the Orchard is the best early investment for its size.[^wiki-spend]
