# Rules

- Unlocks at Farming 40. Each chain takes one item and pays smaller rewards along the way, then a rare final one.
  Donate any amount at a time; progress is kept.[^wiki-temple]
- Regular chains can be reset to do again: each reset costs 10% more, and only one chain can be reset per day.
  Event chains usually last a month, can't be reset, and keep their progress when they come back the next year.[^wiki-temple]
- The planner reads the open chains from a pasted Temple page (Town › Temple of Reward), so seasonal and one-time
  chains count only while the game shows them. Finished chains are skipped.[^player]

# Secret chamber

- A Cursed Effigy opens a chamber with one chain per standard crop: 100,000 of that crop for a Certificate (e.g.
  Certificate of Beet Giving). One time only, no resets, no Double Rewards. 20 of them give the Certified Farmer badge.[^wiki-secret]
- The effigy comes from the "Look, A Secret Temple" questline (after ARK), built from four pieces: Hair ("Effigy of
  Friendship", Buddy friendship 90), Head and Body (Temple of Fate adventure, Tower 10), Gem (end of ARK, Lorn friendship 60).[^wiki-secret]
- Those crop chains would be a big outlet for overflowing crops; the planner uses them once they appear on a pasted
  Temple page.[^player]
