# Places

| Place | Needs |
|---|---|
| Ember Lagoon | Inferno Sphere ("A Way Forward", or craft from Runestones 1-10) + Exploring 60 |
| Lake Minerva | Lava Sphere (Runestones 11-20; "Strange Stones", "Return our lost friend", "Lost and Found") + Fishing 60 |
| Large Island | Tribal Mask ("Islands are Hard to Catch", 11 parts, from Fishing 68) + Fishing 70 |
| Whispering Creek | Compass (Seeing Stone from "Parts Unknown", 24 parts, from Exploring 68 -> Magna Core -> Compass) + Exploring 70 |
| Pirate's Cove | Mapping Compass ("Intruders Beware"; level 6 Bird and Snake pets help) + Fishing 80 |
| Jundland Desert | Exploring 80 (manual only, sandwyrm steals stamina); Y73841 Detector for AP/AC (Fellow Farmer -> Hard at Work -> Unlikely Pair -> Unexpected Visitor) |
| Glacier Lake | Water Orb ("A Wall in the Way", Farming 91 + Fishing 90, and "The Fall of Water"; needs Gold Potato, Piece of Heart, Gold Boot) + Fishing 90 |
| Gary's Crushroom | Key ("Fitness Friends Fit Forever", friendship 80 with Gary Bearson) + Exploring 90 |
| Sinking Swamp | Swamp puzzle pieces |
| The Tower | a level-99 skill + "A Towering Investment" |

Keep one of each sphere in storehouse storage; "trust the quest" when one asks for yours back.[^wiki-rare]
