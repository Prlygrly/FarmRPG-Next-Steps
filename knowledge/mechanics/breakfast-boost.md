# Rules

- Crops whose **official** growth time is 5 minutes or less are ready instantly while it lasts: Carrot, Peas, Cucumber, Eggplant, Peppers and their Gold versions. Radish (10 min) is not one.[^buddy-bb]
- Lasts 2 minutes. One harvest = Plant All + Harvest All (two clicks).[^player]
- Harvests per Boost depends on the player (planner default 250).[^player]
- These crops are farmed as a batch under Boost, not on their timers; it's active work, not passive, so it ranks after regular farming.[^player]
