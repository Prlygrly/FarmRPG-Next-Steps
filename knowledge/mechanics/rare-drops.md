# Super rares

- Some drops are guessed at 1 in 50,000 or rarer, e.g. Langstaff Crest (Jundland), Diamond (Ember Lagoon), Amber (Misty Forest), Raptor Claw (Whispering Creek), Gold Boot (Large Island, manual fishing).[^wiki-super]
- Secondary sources: level 6 pets (Snake: Skull Coin; Lemur: Amber; Pet Rock/Fox: Diamond; Seal: Frost Snapper Shell), Borgen on Wednesdays, Community Center rewards; Langstaff Crest also from "A Wall In The Way".[^wiki-super]

# Runestones

- No official drop rates; can take days, weeks or thousands of crops.[^wiki-rare]
- Expeditions/Charters can't find them; nets and Lemonade can. Crop runestones come from harvesting the crop (Harvest All perk doesn't change it).[^wiki-rare]
- Double Prizes doesn't raise the rate, but can give two at once.[^wiki-rare]
