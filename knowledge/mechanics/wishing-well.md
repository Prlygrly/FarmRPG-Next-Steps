# Rules

- 3 free tosses a day; Farm Supply perks Extra Wish (+1), Extra Wishes (+5), II (+10), III (+10) = 29; beta testers +1 (30). Each toss after that costs 1 gold. Resets at daily reset.[^wiki-ww]
- Odds per input are in [wishing well data](/data/wishing-well.md).[^wiki-wants]
- Tossing a Compass gives a Magna Core back 100% of the time; with Resource Saver, Compass needs become a craft-and-toss loop.[^wiki-compass]

# Planner implications

- Never suggest paid (gold) tosses; show **only today's** free tosses, each with why.[^player]
- Don't toss for things your planned exploring brings anyway, or that are cheaper the usual way; "only the well gives it" means no recipe and no drop anywhere.[^player]
- Stock-up target is the biggest single quest step's need (capped), not a whole chain added up.[^player]
