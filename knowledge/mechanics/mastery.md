# Rules

- Tiers: 100 (Tier I), 1,000 (Tier II), 10,000 (Mastery), 100,000 (Grand Mastery, GM), 1,000,000 (Mega Mastery, MM). Same for every item.[^player]
- Counts every item gained from any source **except mail from other players**.[^player]
- Counted **the moment the item is gained**. Using, tossing, selling or crafting it away afterwards never costs mastery.[^player]
- Items gained past the inventory cap void but **still count**. See [inventory cap](/mechanics/inventory-cap.md).[^player]
- Mastery gives AK towards the [Tower](/mechanics/tower.md): Mastery = 10 AK, GM = 100 AK. Before the Tower opens, the silver, gold and perk point rewards matter more than AK.

# Rewards

| Count | Tier | Rewards |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | (no tier) | 1,000 silver |
| 100 | Tier I | 10,000 silver |
| 1,000 | Tier II | 100,000 silver |
| 10,000 | Mastery (Tier III) | 1,000,000 silver, 5 gold, 1 perk point, sells for 10% more, 10 AK, counts on profile |
| 100,000 | Grand Mastery (Tier IV) | 250,000,000 silver, 25 gold, sells for another 10% more, 100 AK |
| 1,000,000 | Mega Mastery (Tier V) | 2,500,000,000 silver (no AK or gold); each MM lowers what you need for a further reward (see Lunarific's guide); needed for Tower levels over 100 |

- Sell bonus applies after sell perks: 100 silver with 60% perks = 160; mastered x1.1 = 176; GM x1.2 = 192.[^wiki-mastery]
- Rewards are collected from the item's page (Mastery Progress).[^wiki-mastery]
- Not every item has mastery levels.[^wiki-mastery]
- A merit badge comes from Grand Mastering 100 different items.[^wiki-mastery]
- Cooking: tasting only gives bonus mastery once you've had one of that meal in your inventory (a mailed one counts for this, though it doesn't count toward the mastery itself).[^wiki-mastery]

# Planner implications

Never give "keep it for its mastery" as a reason to hold an item; only other uses or value matter.
For players without the Tower, value a mastery by its silver/gold/perk-point reward rather than AK.
