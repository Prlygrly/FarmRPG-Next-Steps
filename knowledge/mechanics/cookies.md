# Rules

- Last 5 minutes; stack as long as they're different cookies.[^player]
- Each seed gives x3 per cookie: 1 cookie x3, 2 cookies x6, 3 cookies x9. Applies to **every** crop, including Breakfast Boost crops.[^player]
- Only worth it when a plain Grape Juice round doesn't already fill your free space (small inventories waste cookies).[^player]
- The player's routine: cookies -> Grape Juice crop -> Boost crops until full -> a crop or two with Veggie Juice down to 1 minute, until the 5 minutes are up.[^player]

# Planner implications

Personal strategy: **never recommend cookies**. At most an optional "cookies I use" setting.
