# Shared Truffle prices (idea, not built)

A Cloudflare Worker + KV (free) would let one player's Steak Market paste give everyone today's Truffle prices.

- Only the two prices and the date are sent; two exact matching reports confirm a day.
- Plausible ranges and a per-device limit guard against false reports.
- r/FarmRPG gets about one Truffle post a week, mostly "% of best value" titles, so a Reddit reader would only catch notable days.
- If built, the daily Steak Market reminder becomes the way to share, and its wording must say the two Truffle prices are shared (knowledge/planner/rules.md).
